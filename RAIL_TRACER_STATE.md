## The train

"Red Line" (working title) is shown only as the start screen's title; the product name, repo, folders and namespaces stay RailTracer.

- **Journey:** three trains of five fixed cars, played in order, the same every playthrough (`Train.cs`; car index 0-14, `Train.TrainCars` / `Trains` / `Cars`). All 26 x 5, you start at the left door. Each car unlocks when the one before it is cleared, so clearing car 5 of a train opens the next train. Every car has a target turn count from the level solver (seed 1).

Then the **walk-through car**: the first carriage's seats, nobody in it, no turns, no move costs, no skills. A glowing tile by the far door asks "Board / Not yet" (Space or Enter boards).
- **After car 5 of train 1 or 2:** the walk-through car's card names the next train and its board panel reads e.g. "The Supper Express · car 1: Pullman car".
- **After car 5 of train 3:** you walk out into the quiet carriage (no board tile), then a card like the tutorial's: "Thanks for playing the demo! If you enjoyed it, please leave a review and join our Discord. Try the Endless mode for more fun!"; any key or click, then "Demo complete": Restart from The Red Line, Try Endless mode, Main menu.
- **Fleeing enemies** jump off the train: broken, gone, never in the next car.
- **Endless mode:** generated cars without end (TrainGen), the same rules; each car 7 to 9 guards and 0 to 2 heavies, rolled from its seed (no climb), and at least 8 seat banks (of about 16 places; `Train.EndlessMinBanks`, missing banks added at random).
- R restarts the current car from its start (the health you came in with; an endless car keeps its layout). N on a won car's card: straight out (debug).

## Saves

- One slot, `save.json` in Unity's persistent data folder, overwritten automatically: at the start of every player turn (after the enemy turn's results, so reloading never rerolls a shot), when a car or walk-through car starts, and when settings change.
- The run is saved as its car, the car's seed, your health coming in and every command since (`Carriage.Do`: moves, leaps, hops, kills, throws, grabs, crouches, undos, each enemy's turn). Continue replays them onto a fresh copy of the car, which lands exactly where you were at the start of that turn: enemies (position, facing, awareness, terror, panic rolls, has-seen), bodies, blood, your stance, health, legend, cooldowns, held body, the turn number and the log. Checked in RulesCheck against 40 random games.
- Also saved: which Journey cars are cleared (15 entries; a save from before 19.21 has 5 and is grown to 15 on load, its progress kept), the best ticket per car (most killed plus broken, then fewest turns) and the settings. The run's car is 0-14 across the trains; Continue reads "train 2, car 3, turn 4".

## Menus

Every menu is over the real carriages: the live (saved) car plus the other cars of its train built beside it, empty and without props (Journey: the picked train's five; the live car sits just before them when it's on another train). Menu train lamps: red for a cleared car, white for the next to play, dark for cars ahead.

- **Layout:** the middle stays clear for the train: the title (or the screen's heading) top left, the menu lines bottom left with any text beside them; no panel behind, the text has a dark glow. No floor overlays behind the menus (reaction zone, routes, arrows, "?" markers, walk range, blood outlines).

## Turn

- 4 moves and 1 action per turn. Crouched or dragging a body costs 2 moves a tile.
- At the start of your turn, anyone not startled who can see you (a searcher that turned your way) is in combat.
- End the turn by holding Space (0.5 s). Undo (Z) until you act, are seen or move a body.
- Your legend (0–100) grows from kills only: stealth kill +10, brutal kill +20, rail grab +30 (Tuning "Quiet Legend", "Brutal Gain", "Rail Gain"). It is also a shield: hits drain it before health.
- Thresholds every 10, notched on the bar at 10–90: brutal unlock 10, hop Bloodied 20, rail unlock 30, stealth Bloodied 40, brutal Bloodied 50, rail Bloodied 60, leap Bloodied 70, demon throw 80, dread 90. Above 90 is overspill: still capped at 100 and still a shield, so it protects dread.
- Health 30. A hit does 15.

Kill details:

- **Stealth kill:** only when the victim can't see you. The victim is pulled onto your tile and dies there; you never move and keep your stance. Heard within 1 tile (the 8 round you, shown as the purple ring on the preview): whoever hears it takes small terror (10) and investigates the noise. Witnesses who see your tile: medium terror (25), combat. Who see only where the victim stood: they vanish (small terror 10; a snatch 35) and they search there.
- **Brutal kill:** a standing act. You move onto the victim's tile; crouched before it, you drop back to crouching after. Heard within 8. Witnesses take large terror (45) and go into combat, except anyone who has never seen you, who is startled. No free shots.
- **Rail grab:** the victim hangs out of their window (or yours, if they're not at one) with one leg inside. Blood on every tile within 1 of the window. Heard within 10 (louder than a brutal kill). Witnesses: large terror (45, or 67 bloodied).
- **Demon throw:** large terror (45) for everyone who sees it land; they investigate the body.
- Toughs (heavies) can't be killed until Shaken.

Chip icons (what they'll do if you end your turn where you are, or where the aimed move ends). Crosshair and charge show only on red (combat) chips and berserk heavies; a searcher keeps its "?":

- **Crosshair:** a guard that sees you and will shoot (not one hesitating, startled or on a panic turn).
- **The swing in the preview:** when the move or leap you're aiming lands where a heavy will reach you and swing, its label reads "Swing · Hit 90%" (it said just "Hit 90%", which read like a reaction shot; its shots are 90% halved to 45% once Shaken, and a berserk heavy is always past Shaken). The swing itself is unchanged: 90% always.
- **The swing:** a heavy's melee plays its "Swing" clip (Editor/AnimationSetup: a Mixamo Punching.fbx in RailTracer/Mixamo when there is one, else the takedown pack's mid-section slash) once it has walked up, and the hit (blood, the red screen, your health going down) lands Game "Swing Hit At" (0.35 s) into it.
- **Charge:** a heavy that sees you, or any berserk heavy. They charge 3 tiles a turn and melee (90%, always: no shaken, cover or leap cut) when next to you; their shots (reaction fire, overwatch) are 90%, cut like a guard's. A charging heavy that gets beside you (a straight neighbour, still seeing you, or berserk) swings at once, which ends its turn; one that would need all 4 steps only closes in. So a heavy threatens any tile within 4 walkable steps (3 to arrive, 1 to swing): its chip shows the charge icon when your tile, or the aimed move's end, is in that range; hover reads "Charge · Hit 90%" (else "Charge"); the move preview highlights it with its hit chance like a shooter.
- **Wild fire:** a panicked guard on a wild fire turn.
- **Ghost on a freeze turn:** a panicked guard frozen with fear (loses the turn, like startled): a small sheet ghost, black on white chips, white on red. Hover: "Frozen with fear: loses the turn".
- **Armour plate** on toughs: whole while calm (can't be killed), cracked once Shaken. Targeting a kill on an unshaken tough shows "Not yet" over it.
- Terror segments: three (Shaken, Panicked, Broken), white outline on red and black chips, black on white. The eye: has seen you (it can't be startled).

Hover label: name, role, one state line ("Searching · Shaken", "Panicked · Freeze", "Berserk", "Fled", "Begging"), and what they'll do next ("Hit 80%", "Charge", "Investigate: Noise / Blood / Body / Movement", "Hold", "Patrol", "Back to post", "Loses the turn").

### Behaviour

- **Unaware:** guards hold their post and turn every 2 turns (an arrow shows the turn before); patrols walk their route; after a search, guards walk back to their post.
- **An unaware enemy who spots you mid-move** turns red but doesn't use reaction fire that turn. **At the start of its turn, if it still sees you, it attacks.**
- **Searching** does one of three things:
  1. **Go to the spot** (a noise, a body, where it saw you).
  2. **Follow a blood trail** to the newest part it can see right now (the "?" marker moves with it).
  - A "?" on the floor marks where a searcher will go and look; none for looking around in place (shout or gunfire) and none on the last-seen ghost.
  3. **Look around** for 3 turns: at the spot, or where it stands if it heard a shout or gunfire. At the spot, each turn it steps at most 2 tiles (Tuning "Sweep Step", within "Search Radius" 2 of the spot), then faces one of the three ways it wasn't facing (rolled at the start of your turn); where it stands, it just turns a new way. (Before 19.8 it could walk 3 tiles anywhere within 2 of the spot and roll the way it already faced, so routes wandered and doubled back.) A floor line runs from the enemy along the route it will walk next turn, ending in an arrow for the way it will face (turning where it stands: just the arrow at its feet). Every enemy that will move or turn shows it (patrols, going back to post, investigating, following a trail, charging, looking around, a guard turning on watch). Since 19.6 the routes come from the enemy turn itself: whenever you act, the game replays the car onto a copy (as a save is restored, the same dice) and plays the coming enemy turn there as if you ended your turn where you stand, so the line is where each one will really go, other enemies' shots, shouts and moves included. It updates as you move.
  Then it gives up and walks back.
- **Shouting:** the first enemy to see you shouts and comes looking itself (it's in combat). Unaware listeners within 6 tiles turn white, face the shout and look around where they stand.
- **Gunfire:** everyone not in combat turns to face it and looks around where they stand (the carriage doesn't pile in).
- **Bodies** stop whoever finds one for that turn. Terror by how they died: stealth 10, brutal or shot 25, rail grab or thrown 45. Log: "Name found Victim's body (brutal)."
- **Blood:** seen like a crouched player on that tile; each enemy reacts once per stain.

### Terror

- Shaken (30+): shots at half their hit chance (guards 40%, heavies 45%); 40% chance each turn to hesitate instead of shooting.
- Panicked (60+), guards: every other turn is a panic turn, starting with the next turn after they panic. On a panic turn they freeze (50%, tunable; lose the turn, no reaction fire that player turn) or fire wildly at a random target in front of them (30% to hit, ally or you; you only if they can see you, so crouched behind a seat you're safe; allies by line of sight). The roll is made at the start of your turn and shown on the chip. Other turns: normal, with the Shaken penalties. No fleeing or charging.
- Panicked (60+), heavies: berserk for good. They charge your real tile every turn (3 tiles), melee when next to you, and never drop to searching.
- Broken (100): one roll: flee to the next carriage (50%, tunable; needs a path to a door, else they beg) or kneel and beg. Both count as broken; killing a broken enemy turns its ticket red. If the level is won while someone is still fleeing, they run out before the end card. A fleer runs right out of the end door (Game "Flee Out Past", 2.5 m beyond the door tile) before it's gone.
- Terror values: small 10, medium 25, large 45, unseen (a body dragged or someone snatched away by something unseen) 35. A leap landing seen: legend × 40 / 100. Jump scare 15: an enemy that starts its turn right beside you (any of the 8 tiles) without seeing you, and ends it seeing you (it turned on watch, or walked into you), is scared (log: "Name turns and finds you right beside them. They jump!").
- Enemy routes don't go round you: they don't know you're there. A walk whose next step is your tile stops there; the enemy turns to you and, unless already fighting, fights you (log: "Name walks right into you!"). Fleeing broken enemies still run round you.

## Sight

- Range: guards 7, heavies 8. Never changes with awareness.
- Guards: a 90° wedge in front (full range), plus two tiles of side vision one row ahead.
- Heavies: the same, plus the first 2 tiles straight out to each side.
- **Partitions** (a column walled off but for its doorway, like Third class's): a sight line through one has to pass the doorway, where the wall is drawn (on the joint after the partition's tiles), within 0.45 m of its middle; so from a steep angle only about a tile shows through, and straight down the aisle is clear. Throws and the other line-of-sight checks follow the same rule.
- Each standing enemy on the line halves the sight left beyond them (broken ones and bodies don't count).
- Luggage and walls block sight. Seats block it only for something low (you crouched, a body, blood).
- **Half cover (crouched):** a seat on the line to the viewer hides you if it's right next to you (viewer 3 tiles away or less) or within 2 tiles of you (viewer further). Worked out per viewer. Bodies and blood use the same rule.
- **Close look:** a searcher spots you on any tile next to it inside its cone, cover or not (a guard's 3 in front, a heavy's 5).

## Reaction fire

- From searching and combat enemies with their shot unspent this turn (not on a panicked guard's freeze turn).
- Triggers on stepping onto a tile they see you on (not the tile you start on), every tile of a leap's arc and its landing, a hop's landing tile (shown once you've climbed back in: the tracers, the red hit flash and blood, and the health drop all wait for the hop), and standing up. Once per enemy per turn. A shot (hit or miss) doesn't stop a walk: you finish the move unless it kills you (so a walk past several ready guns can draw a shot from each; the preview marks each shooter's tile).
- Shots at a leaping player are 25 points lower (a guard's 80% becomes 55%).
- Shown on the floor as a faint red tint on every watched tile (Game "Zone Tint", 0.12) and a fine red dashed line (8 dashes a tile) round exactly the red tiles: every edge between a watched tile and one that isn't. The move preview marks the tile in red and highlights the shooter with its hit chance.

## Hits

- Shots: guards 80%, heavies 90% (Tuning hitChance, heavyHitChance); shaken or worse ×0.5 (shakenHitMult); half cover ×0.75; leap −25. A heavy's melee 90% flat. Wild fire 30%.

## Characters

- **The guards:** two NYC1950 models (made by `Editor/CharacterPackSetup.cs`, each with an 11-bone ragdoll from RagdollSetup): **CH01** (Resources/Anim/Guard.prefab: fedora, cream shirt sleeves, trousers, a brass chain and watch) and **CH02** (Guard_CH02.prefab: fedora, double-breasted suit, cream shirt and tie, dark glasses, a brass ring). **Heavies wear CH02**, so their red covers the whole suit; guards alternate by id (even CH02, odd CH01). A missing model falls back to the other, then to the Mixamo character.
  - CH02 came from Alex's UE 4.22 project (NYC_Character02), exported from a copy (UE 5.6 upgrades it in memory; the Downloads copy untouched). Its split is by section: each clothes section of the skinned mesh (the suit section holds jacket and trousers; another shoes, shirt with tie and ring; hat; glasses) takes the nearest static part among the parts that make it up, so the jacket can't take the shirt under it. New material Chr_Glasses (near black, glossy). `Assets/NYC1950_CH02/Meshes/SK_CH02_FullClothes.fbx`, gitignored like CH01.
  - CH01 source: Alex's Unreal project (NYC_Character01, UE 5.6), SK_CH01_FullClothes on the Unreal mannequin skeleton (67 bones), exported by a headless editor run (Python), then split in Blender so each face carries one of our material names (the pack's clothes materials are atlases shared by several parts; each face takes the nearest static part's material). Saved as `Assets/NYC1950_CH01/Meshes/SK_CH01_FullClothes.fbx`, imported Humanoid (hips = pelvis, toes = ball). 26k faces.
  - The pack (`Assets/NYC1950_CH01`) is gitignored like the other store packs; Guard.prefab needs it.
  - Both skeletons are in metres at scale 1 (the Unreal export came in centimetres under a 0.01 root scale; Unity's Ragdoll Builder then made the head's sphere under a millimetre across in that space, so the heads glitched as they died). The guard prefabs are rebuilt with fresh ragdolls.
  - **Our materials, not its 4k textures:** hat Trn_Felt, chain and watch Trn_Brass, shirt and vest Chr_Shirt (cream) and trousers Chr_Suit (charcoal), both copies of Fabric.mat's weave; flat Chr_Skin, Chr_Hair, Chr_Eye, Chr_Mouth, Chr_Shoes in the game's own tones. Chr_ materials in `Characters/Materials`, made once, never overwritten. The beard and brows are alpha cards, so without their texture they read as blocky stubble up close.
  - **Type colours:** the suit tint (black guards, red heavies, grey broken) goes on Chr_Suit (CH01's trousers, CH02's jacket and trousers); hair and shoes take the game's hair and shoe colours; the guards' blue / green eyes colour the model's own eyes (Chr_Eye), since it has no Mixamo lash mesh to place iris discs from. Game.Colour now matches material names too on a mesh with several materials.
- The static T-pose parts (SM_CH01_*) are dressed the same way (by file) but unused.
- **Batch check:** `Unity -batchmode -projectPath . -executeMethod RailTracer.CharacterPackSetup.Check -quit` sets up every kit's materials, the pack and the guard, and logs the avatar, bones, materials and ragdoll.

## Camera, HUD and presentation

- Kill cam on every takedown, brutal and stealth (as long as the finisher), rail grabs and throws (1.2 s): the camera flies in (0.45 s), holds a beat (0.2 s), plays the shot from a fixed angle, then flies back to your exact view (0.35 s). The angle is low, side-on and tight on the pair (Kill Cam Close 1.25), trying other close angles until no wall, floor, luggage or seat back is in the way ; it pulls back only when every close angle is blocked. A takedown's own shot (Cam Offset / Cam Look) comes first: seat backs don't count against it, only walls, floor and luggage; if one is in the way the same shot (height, distance, look point) swings round, mirrored first, then 30 degrees at a time, and only if none is clear do the defaults take over. Slow motion: the Options setting (Game "Kill Cam Slow Mo"), one value for every kill cam, only for Game "Hit Slow Time" (0.5 s, real time) on each hit it shows: a takedown's Blood At and every extra blood point, a rail grab's impact as the shot starts, a thrown body as it lands. Everything else in the shot plays at full speed; takedowns keep their own Speed. How often a kill gets the kill cam: Options "Kill cam chance". The camera controls are off during it (it keeps exactly the shot it was given). It can't be skipped; Options and the pause menu: "Kill cam: On/Off" (default On), and its slow motion in Options. The HUD hides during it.
- **Bloody footprints** (looks only): from legend 50 (Game "Footprints From"), walking leaves shoe prints where the animated feet land (since 19.16: a print under each foot as it comes down, between its ankle and toes, pointing the way it points; without foot bones, Game "Footprint Stride" 0.42 m and "Footprint Gap" 0.09 m either side of your line; "Footprint Length" 0.32 m, "Footprint Width" 0.115 m; since 19.13 a classic shoe print: an egg-shaped sole leaning to the inside, a slanted gap, a heel set to the inside). Since 19.8 they are the blood's own decals: a copy of the RVFX blood decal material (lit, glossy) with a shoe-print texture in the blood texture's red (Game "Footprint Color", about #731F1F, from the blood texture's average; flat normals), projected down "Footprint Depth" (0.5 m) onto whatever is there, the aisle runner and bodies included (the flat quads they replaced sat under the runner). They stay for the rest of the car (since 19.12 an undo removes the ones laid this turn). Also on the walk out of a cleared car (19.15). Not on leaps, hops or takedown slides; none in the walk-through car.
- **On the aisle runner:** the kit floor's velvet runner (1 m wide down the middle of every kit car) stands 1.5 cm proud of the floor; you and the enemies stand on it (Game "Runner Height", 0.015), eased in over its edges (from 0.62 m to 0.38 m off the centreline). Bodies and takedown victims don't lift.
- Demon aura (Resources/Effects/Demon Aura) round your feet: fades in with the same eased legend as the blood on your clothes, from nothing at 10 legend to the prefab's full colours at 100 (Game "Aura From" 0.1, "Aura Full" 1).
- The carriage lamps redden with legend, stepping at 25, 50, 75 and 100 and blending over about a second. Overlays never change.
